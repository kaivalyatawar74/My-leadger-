💰 My Ledger App

Manage Your Money. Track Every Transaction.

My Ledger is a simple and user-friendly application designed to help users manage their daily financial records efficiently.

✨ Features

- 💸 Track money received and paid
- 👥 Maintain person-wise transaction records
- 📊 Record daily expenses
- 📒 Manage personal accounts in one place
- 🔐 PIN-based app access
- 📱 Simple and easy-to-use interface

🎯 Purpose

The goal of My Ledger is to make everyday money management simple, organized, and convenient.
